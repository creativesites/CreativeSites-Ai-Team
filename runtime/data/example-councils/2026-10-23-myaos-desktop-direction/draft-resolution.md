# Draft resolution (as adopted after Round 3)

**Recommendation:** Option A, Mission Control, cut to one job. Verdict: GO WITH CONDITIONS.

The MyaOS desktop app takes one real job end to end: an outcome in, one agent works in its own worktree, the verifier checks observed results, a person approves before merge. The runtime is the one store for task data. Everything else in the app waits.

**The cash rule.** Total cash out from this week, build and burn together, is capped at $60k. The founder confirms the real monthly burn this week. If that burn makes the cap unworkable, the answer is B. Pilots are a gate, not a funding source.

**Conditions (each checkable):**
1. Store decision signed this week: the runtime owns task data. One page, one name. (Bezos, Graham, Wickman)
2. Founder writes down real monthly burn and cash on hand this week. If burn is above $40k a month and runway is under four months, the answer is B. (Buffett, Hormozi, Bezos)
3. A named Integrator who is not the founder owns the week-8 Scorecard and runs GWC before the store spike starts. (Wickman)
4. Store migration spike finishes in under two weeks, with the runtime as the only task owner, before any worktree work starts. One runtime change ships by someone other than the founder within those two weeks. (Elon, Buffett, Wickman)
5. Week 6, the build gate: at least one named developer team pays $1,500 in cash, no deposits. (Buffett, Bezos, Elon, Wickman)
6. Week 6, the demand gate: at least three developers who are not friends of the founders have paid for a verification report. Build spend past the store spike waits for this count. (Graham)
7. Scale gate: five named teams paid in cash is the price of spending past the $60k cap. (Hormozi, Elon, Bezos)
8. Week 8: one real repo runs the loop end to end with one agent. The Scorecard shows captured diff and test evidence, checked by a second agent. Week-8 cash is on the Scorecard every week from week one. (Bezos, Elon, Wickman)
9. Week 1: delete or label as demo the fake 35% progress, the hard-coded coworkers, and the invented dashboard numbers. (Elon, Wickman, Bezos)
10. Ten developer conversations start this week, in parallel with the build. (Graham, Elon)

**Next steps with dates** (today is 23 October 2026):
1. By 30 October 2026: store decision signed, Integrator named, burn and cash written down, fake data removed, developer conversations started. (founder)
2. By 6 November 2026: store migration spike done, and one runtime change shipped by someone other than the founder, or cut to B. (Integrator)
3. By 4 December 2026 (week 6): build gate (one team paid $1,500 cash) and demand gate (three report payers). Either missing means B. (founder)
4. By 18 December 2026 (week 8): one real repo runs the loop end to end. Week-8 cash at least $40k. Continue or drop to B. (founder and Integrator)

**Cheapest test:** Run one real Claude Code task by hand through the app. Capture the diff and test exit status. Send the report to ten developers this week and ask one of them for a card number.

**Kill criteria:**
- No paid team at week 6: drop to B (Buffett, Hormozi, Wickman).
- Store spike runs past two weeks, or no runtime change ships without the founder: drop to B (Elon, Buffett, Wickman).
- Real burn above $40k a month and runway under four months: drop to B (Buffett, Hormozi).
- Fewer than three non-friend developers pay for the report by week 6: stop building past the spike and rerun the council (Graham, Bezos).
- Three developers will not pay for a verification report within 30 days: move the wedge to C as the product (Elon, Graham).

**Minority positions carried:**
- Hormozi wants five paid teams before build continues past week 6. The resolution makes five the price of spending past the cap, not the week-6 gate.
- Graham wants the report sold before any store work. The resolution counts three report payers at week 6 before build spend past the spike.
