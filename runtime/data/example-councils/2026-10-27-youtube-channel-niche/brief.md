# Brief: which YouTube niche to commit to

**Decision.** Should a solo founder start an AI-narrated YouTube channel, and if so, which niche does he commit to? Switching niches later costs subscribers, so the choice is close to one-way.

**Options**
- **A.** Hybrid: one channel, "science and history, told as stories," with space and science "what if" as the lead format. (Founder's lean.)
- **B.** Space and science "what if" only (black holes, quantum weirdness, "what if the Moon vanished").
- **C.** Untold history of science and space (the human drama behind discoveries and missions).
- **D.** Comedy-tinged AI and tech stories (funny narrative takes on tech history and AI mishaps).
- **E.** Keep doing what I do now. Full time on Odoo products and client work, no channel. (Don't do it.)
- **F.** 90-day pilot first: one lean format, 4 to 6 videos, kill criteria set before the first upload, then commit or drop.

**Business.** Solo founder in Lusaka, building Odoo-based SaaS products and doing client work. AI tools do all production. Stage: early. Monthly revenue: under $1,000 (founder's answer).

**Resources.**
- Cash for the channel: under $500 total.
- Tool spend (voice, images, video, editing): under $50 a month.
- Founder hours: 6 to 10 a week. Some of that happens from a phone.
- First video live in 4 weeks.

**Success (assumed from the founder's example, not confirmed).** Monetized within 6 months. 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 watch hours within 12 months (YouTube Partner Program bar). Some monthly revenue by month 12 (amount unstated). The channel must not take time from the product work.

**Format.** 30 to 40 minute narrated videos. AI images, some AI video clips, AI narration. Every video needs an original script and varied visuals, since YouTube does not monetize template-driven or mass-produced AI content.

**Research the founder cites (vendor blogs, directional only).**
- Story-driven history and dark history: about $10 to 15 RPM, moderate growth.
- True crime documentaries: 70 to 82% retention, slow to 1K subscribers.
- "What if" scenarios: fastest to 1K subscribers, about $6 to 11 RPM.
- Horror: fast early growth, about $4 to 8 RPM.

**Founder's worries to test.** (1) Space and science is crowded. (2) Factual errors would damage trust. (3) A mixed channel confuses the algorithm. Is one niche better than the hybrid?

**What the council should answer.** The best option, the verdict label, the cheapest test before committing, and the kill criteria.
