**Rulings on the amendments.** Each one is adopted, adopted in part, or put in the minority report.

1. **Bezos: automatic stop on Odoo billing down more than 10% at day 90.** Adopted. Buffett's card already had this stop, so no seat objects.
2. **Graham: day-60 stop when median video is under 300 views or average view duration under 35%, regardless of inquiries.** Adopted. The draft joined the two with AND, which let a channel that failed on viewers survive on one inquiry. Graham's day-14 test was already in the draft's conditions: 100 named by day 14, under 30 means stop. The draft's table row carried the 30 floor only, so the row now reads both.
3. **Hormozi: test unit of ten or more offer-linked pieces.** Adopted in part. The test unit is pieces, not long videos: each long video is cut into native pieces, each carrying a paid Odoo link. Gary V's six per video covers Hormozi's ten. The room's five-videos count is dropped (see 5).
4. **Hormozi: day 90 continues only at five signed clients.** Not adopted at day 90. Five clients is 1:1 on hours, and that is the right bar for a second 90 days. Day 90 is a read, so the bar there is one signed client (Buffett's amendment). Five signed clients is the day-180 continue bar. Hormozi's objection is in the minority report.
5. **Martell and Hormozi: four long videos, not five.** Adopted. Five videos at 10 hours each is 50 hours, which leaves 2 hours of a 52-hour pilot for the cuts. Four videos at 10 hours is 40 hours, leaving about 10 hours for roughly 24 cuts at Gary V's 25 minutes each. The four-video plan fits the hours; the five-video plan does not.
6. **Gary V: cuts are the test unit, six per long video, read against the channel median at day 60.** Adopted. Hours are logged in the same cap.
7. **Gary V and Martell: 3-hour cap, not 4.** Not adopted. Four seats (Bezos, Buffett, Hormozi, Naval) and Graham's card set 4. The cuts sit inside the 4-hour cap, as Gary V asked, so the cap is four hours with cuts counted in it. Gary V's and Martell's 3-hour view is in the minority report.
8. **Buffett: day-90 bar is one signed Odoo client, not an inquiry.** Adopted. An inquiry is not revenue and costs nothing to count.
9. **Buffett: log hours against money, Odoo rate written down.** Adopted. Already in the draft's before-upload list.
10. **Naval: day-90 continue rule requires a fixed-price Odoo product, not a custom project.** Adopted for the day-180 bar. The second 90 days require five signed clients, at least the first of them on a fixed-price product. The day-90 bar stays one signed client of any kind. Naval's objection that one project sale is labor sold through media is in the minority report.
11. **Martell: day-60 gate, no paid Odoo inquiry traced to the channel means stop.** Adopted. It sits alongside the day-45 hour and billing check.

**Minority report.**
- Hormozi: one signed client is $320 against $1,560 of forgone work, so day 90 does not pay for the hours. He would have the first continue bar at five clients.
- Naval: a project sale is labor sold through media, so the second sale costs as many hours as the first. He would make the product the only continue bar.
- Gary V and Martell: a 3-hour cap is the honest number, given the founder's whole week and the Odoo hours.
- Bezos (Round 2): four hours a week over 13 weeks is 52 hours, enough for five videos. The Chair's count with cuts is four, so the hours fit. Five stays open if the two-week audit shows first videos at 8 hours or under. No seat has yet said that in writing.

**Final resolution.** F, with the adopted amendments:
- One channel under "science and history, told as stories," with "what if" as the labelled lead on about 80% of pieces. Founder build stories on about 20%. Every piece tagged by format.
- Four long videos, each cut into native pieces (about six per video, if the audit shows a cut takes 25 minutes or less). Every piece links a paid Odoo offer.
- 4 hours a week, cuts included, logged in 15-minute blocks, Odoo client work scheduled first.
- Two-week audit before the first script. First video over 10 hours drops the pilot to two long videos.
- Cash cap $150 over 90 days. Tool spend under $50 a month.
- Kill and switch rules: day 14, fewer than 30 people named stops. Day 45, Odoo billing down more than 10% pauses. Day 60, median under 300 views or average view duration under 35% stops; no traced paid inquiry stops; fewer than two videos at 1,000 views switches the lead to history. Day 90, billing down more than 10% stops; no signed Odoo client stops. Day 180, five signed clients, at least the first on a fixed-price product, to continue.
