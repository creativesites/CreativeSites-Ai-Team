## Bezos

SEAT: BEZOS (Strategy and decisions)
STANCE: Conditional yes
OPTION SCORES: [A] 4/10 · [B] 7/10 · [C] 6/10 · [D] 3/10 · [E] 1/10 · [F] 9/10
PILLAR SCORE: 7/10
CONFIDENCE: 3/5
RUIN RISK: possible
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: Run F as a 90-day pilot of the space and science "what if" format (B), with kill criteria written before the first upload, and defer the niche decision to day 90.

WHY:
1. The niche is the hard door to walk back through, so I won't pick it on a guess. The pilot is the cheap door. "Type 2 decisions can and should be made quickly by high judgment individuals or small groups." (Type 1 and Type 2 decisions, S063). Four to six videos cost under $500 and roughly 80 to 130 founder hours (my assumption: 6 to 10 hours a week for 13 weeks). Test first, then make the irreversible call with data.
2. Hybrid A fails the test design. Mixing formats means no single result tells you which niche works, and the founder's worry about the algorithm is right. "be as narrow and as focused as is possible to be" (Focus versus diversification, S018). B is narrow enough to read.
3. Customer test (Start with the customer and work backwards, S056). In ten years, viewers still want a story that makes them see how the universe works. That need is stable. The topic is the variable, so I'd test the topic and keep the storytelling fixed.
4. Runway is where my lens misfires. Under $1,000 a month in revenue means Odoo is the bank account. The hours are the real cost, not the $50 tool budget. "I don't believe in bet the company bets that's when you're desperate" (S018). The channel is a bet you can afford only if it stays small. Cap it at 10 hours a week and protect client work first.

Kill criteria (my proposed numbers, not from the brief): at day 90 after four uploads, kill if total views are under 2,000, if hours exceed 10 a week for three weeks, or if billable Odoo hours fall more than 20 percent.

THE RISK I SEE: Hours leak from client work, Odoo revenue drops, and the pilot becomes a slow drain with no decision at day 90.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: If the first two videos each pass 1,000 views in 30 days with rising retention, I would commit to B early. If Odoo revenue is under $500 a month with no contracted work, I would stop the channel and fix revenue first.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Write the kill criteria on one page, with a view count, an hour cap, and an Odoo revenue floor, and date them before the first script.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: What was your Odoo revenue over the last three months, and how much of the next six months is already contracted?

## Graham

STANCE: Conditional yes
OPTION SCORES: [A] 3/10 · [B] 5/10 · [C] 5/10 · [D] 4/10 · [E] 2/10 · [F] 8/10

CONFIDENCE: 2/5
RUIN RISK: none
DOOR: two-way

THE CALL: Run F, a 90-day pilot of one "what if" format (B's style), with kill criteria written down before the first upload.

WHY:
1. Nobody has watched this channel yet, so demand is unknown. "You have to make something people want, and you prosper only to the extent you do." (S092) Four to six videos is the cheapest way to get a real reaction. The brief's niche rankings come from vendor blogs, so they tell you nothing about your audience.
2. The money at the success bar is small. I'm assuming a 7-minute average view on 30 to 40 minute videos (not in the brief). 4,000 watch hours is 240,000 minutes, so about 34,000 views. At $6 to $11 RPM that is roughly $200 to $400 in a year. Your Odoo revenue is already under $1,000 a month. The channel is an asset bet, not income. Its hours are a fixed cost, and "The fatal pinch is default dead + slow growth + not enough time to fix it." (S080) At 6 to 10 hours a week, the channel cannot take the product hours.
3. A mixed channel cannot show you what is growing. "you can use growth like a compass to make almost every decision you face." (S100) Hybrid A points the compass at three formats at once. "You got to start with a small intense fire." (S010) Pick one format, measure it weekly, and let the numbers choose.
Concession: "Most people should not try to start startups." (S100) This is a side channel, not a venture startup, so the growth reflex is weaker here. I still use it, because the test costs under $500.

THE RISK I SEE: The 90 days become a sunk cost. Odoo client work slips, the videos get modest views, and nobody agrees whether that counted as growth.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: A pre-existing audience of 300 or more people who already ask for these videos would make this a strong yes. At day 60, if the median video is under 300 views and average view duration is under 35 percent, I drop it. Those thresholds are my assumptions, not from the brief.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Write the format's kill criteria in a dated note, then script the first video.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: Who are the first 100 people who would be upset if this channel stopped, and where do they already gather?

3. One channel is already a new variable. A hybrid (A) adds a second format, and under $1M every added variable is a strike (111 rule, S059). C earns the format slot on the brief's numbers, and original research protects it from the mass-produced AI rule.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: One signed Odoo client sourced from the channel within 90 days moves me to Yes. Zero paid leads from six videos, or RPM under $6 across the first three, kills it.

WHAT I'D DO THIS WEEK: Write down your last Odoo project's price and delivery hours, then put a paid Odoo offer link in the description of video one.

QUESTION FOR THE FOUNDER: What does your average Odoo project pay, and how many hours does it take you to deliver?

## Chair notes

**Unanimity check.** All seven seats name F as their top option. Their F scores sit between 8 and 9, so the Round 1 spread is within one point. The Chair treats this as a warning, not a win.

**Strongest unargued alternative.** The founder's own lean, A (the hybrid), scored 2 to 5 across the table and no seat argued it at full strength. Nobody made the case that one channel with a consistent umbrella beats a single-format pilot. The seats whose pillars are most at risk from A are Gary V (attention and distribution: a mixed channel may reach more people faster) and Graham (real demand: a mixed channel may find demand sooner). Both must argue A at full strength in Round 2.

**Cruxes.** Three open disagreements, written to `cruxes.json`:
1. Which format leads the pilot: space and science "what if" (B) or untold history (C). Bezos, Martell and Gary V lean B; Buffett and Hormozi lean C; Naval wants his own AI-builder voice (D) inside the pilot.
2. Whether the channel earns anything beyond attention. Gary V and Bezos say attention is the asset worth a cheap test. Hormozi, Buffett and Naval say the views earn about $12 to $22 a month, so the channel only pays if it feeds a paid Odoo offer.
3. How many hours the pilot may take. Martell caps it at 2 hours a week, Gary V at about 3, Buffett at 4, Hormozi at 8, and Bezos at 10. The hours per video differ too: Martell assumes 8, Hormozi and Buffett 10.

**Assumptions the cards used that the brief did not give.** Average view length is 7 minutes (Graham) or 10 minutes (Naval). Odoo billing is $25 an hour (Martell), $30 an hour (Hormozi and Naval). Those numbers differ, so Round 2 needs one number for each, with the seat that chose it named.

**Card format.** All seven cards follow the shape in `round-prompts.md`. Nothing was sent back. Ruin risk is "possible" for Bezos and Hormozi, and "none" for the other five, so the ruin cap does not apply.
