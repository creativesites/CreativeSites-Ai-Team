# Draft resolution

**Recommendation.** Option F: a 90-day pilot of one channel, run under the "science and history, told as stories" umbrella. The "what if" format is the labelled lead. Nothing is committed beyond day 90. The channel is a bet the founder can stop, not a new business.

**Verdict label.** GO WITH CONDITIONS. Option F scores 8.0 across seats (Round 2, weighted). The conditions below are what keep it from being a free pass.

## What the pilot is

- **Channel shape.** One channel. Every long video sits under the umbrella. Roughly 80% of pieces lead with a "what if" story (Bezos, Gary V, Graham). Roughly 20% are the founder's own Odoo build stories, documented, not invented (Gary V, Naval). Every video is tagged by format so results can be read by format at day 60 (Graham, Gary V).
- **Lead switch.** If two videos have not reached 1,000 views each by day 60, the lead switches to untold history (C) for the next 30 days (Bezos, Buffett, Hormozi's format pick). The umbrella stays.
- **Test unit.** Five long videos, 30 to 40 minutes each, over 90 days. Shorts cut from them are a distribution bonus, not the test unit, and only count if hours allow (Bezos, Buffett). Hormozi's 50-piece target is dropped: it does not fit 4 hours a week at 10 hours per long video (see Open numbers).
- **Offer on every video.** Each video links a paid Odoo offer, a fixed-price product where one exists, and a project otherwise (Hormozi, Naval, Buffett, Martell).
- **Fact rule.** Every factual claim has a named primary source and a second check before upload. A failed episode is pulled (Bezos, Buffett).

## Hours and money

- **Hours.** 4 hours a week, hard cap, logged in 15-minute blocks. Odoo client work is scheduled first (Bezos, Buffett, Hormozi, Naval, Martell's block-first rule).
- **Two-week audit first.** Before the first script, log the real hours for two weeks. Martell's first-video estimate is 10 hours. If the first video runs over 10 hours, the pilot drops to two videos (Martell).
- **Cash cap.** $150 across the 90 days (Buffett). Tool spend stays under $50 a month, the founder's own limit.
- **Hour cost.** Write the Odoo billing rate at the top of the log this week. Martell's rule: if the rate is under $30 an hour, raise the client price before the first upload. If it is over $50, the channel drops to one video a month.

## Before the first upload (all required)

1. Kill criteria written in a dated file (Bezos, Gary V, Naval, Hormozi).
2. Odoo billing rate written down (Buffett, Martell).
3. Two-week hour log started (Martell).

## Kill and switch rules

| When | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Day 14 | Fewer than 30 people named who would be upset if the channel stopped (Graham's threshold, assumed) | Stop the channel |
| Day 45 | Odoo billing down more than 10% from the three-month baseline | Pause the channel (Buffett, Bezos) |
| Day 60 | Fewer than two videos have reached 1,000 views each | Switch the lead to C for the next 30 days (Bezos) |
| Day 60 | Median video under 300 views and no paid Odoo inquiry | Stop the channel (Graham, Gary V) |
| Day 60 | One piece at 5x the channel's baseline | Keep the format and cut the rest of the plan around it (Gary V) |
| Any week | Channel hours over 4 for three straight weeks | Pause the channel (Buffett) |
| Day 90 | No qualified Odoo inquiry from the channel's links | Stop. No second vote (Buffett, Bezos, Hormozi) |
| Day 90 | One signed Odoo client sourced from the channel | Continue to the next 90 days, on the same hour cap (Hormozi, Naval) |

## Open numbers the Chair has set

- **Hours per long video: 10.** Martell, Hormozi, Buffett and Bezos's cap all point to 10 for a first format. Martell's audit confirms it by day 14. Gary V's 25-minute shorts are an untested assumption, not a planning number.
- **Piece unit: long videos.** Hormozi's 50 pieces and Gary V's 10 shorts per pillar do not fit 52 hours. The Chair uses five long videos as the test.
- **Average view length: 7 minutes or 10?** Graham assumed 7, Naval assumed 10. The Chair uses 10 minutes, the same as Naval's. Day 60 replaces it with the real number.

## Cheapest test

The two-week hour log, then one long video with a paid Odoo link. Both cost under $150 and about 20 founder hours, and both can be stopped at no cost.

## Kill criteria (summary)

Day 90: no qualified Odoo inquiry from the channel, or Odoo billing down more than 10%. Either one ends the channel.

## Revisit

Day 60 (format and lead decision, with real view and retention numbers) and day 90 (continue or stop, with the Odoo inquiry count and billing).
